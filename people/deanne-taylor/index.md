Director of Bioinformatics, Children’s Hospital of Philadelphia

Research Associate Professor of Pediatrics, Perelman School of Medicine, University of Pennsylvania

Dr. Taylor’s research includes knowledge graphs, developmental biology and computational approaches to human disease, alongside work in AI/ML, physics and biophysics. She holds a BA in physics and astronomy and an MS and PhD in biophysics.
